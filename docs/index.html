1. Dense Traffic Two-Stage Pipeline

In high-density traffic scenes (such as Indian arterial corridors with multiple overlapping cars, commercial cabs, and trucks), direct number plate detectors struggle with false positives caused by vehicle logos, advertising decals, and rear-window text.

Our two-stage architecture solves this by enforcing a spatial hierarchy:

2. Coordinate Mapping & Training Formulas

Let $(X_{v1}, Y_{v1}, X_{v2}, Y_{v2})$ denote the vehicle bounding box coordinates in the original frame. Let $(x_{p1}, y_{p1}, x_{p2}, y_{p2})$ denote the license plate coordinates within the vehicle crop: 

Global Plate Coordinate:
X_global = X_v1 + x_p1
Y_global = Y_v1 + y_p1

YOLO Training Format (Normalized [0.0 - 1.0]):
c_x = ((X1 + X2) / 2.0) / Image_Width
c_y = ((Y1 + Y2) / 2.0) / Image_Height
w   = (X2 - X1) / Image_Width
h   = (Y2 - Y1) / Image_Height
