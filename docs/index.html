1. Dense Traffic Two-Stage Pipeline

In high-density traffic scenes (such as Indian arterial corridors with multiple overlapping cars, commercial cabs, and trucks), direct number plate detectors struggle with false positives caused by vehicle logos, advertising decals, and rear-window text.

Our two-stage architecture solves this by enforcing a spatial hierarchy that isolates the vehicle boundary first, and then accurately extracts the high-resolution license plate image crop:

2. Why Two-Stage Vehicle & Plate Localization?

Direct single-stage plate detectors struggle with false positives caused by bumper stickers, brand emblems, and background road signs. By first detecting the vehicle boundary and then localizing the license plate inside the vehicle ROI:

  • Eliminates Roadside False Positives: Removes false detections on roadside billboards, building signs, or irrelevant background clutter.
  • Isolates High-Resolution Crops: Provides clean, dedicated bounding box crops for each vehicle and its corresponding license plate for standalone inspection and archiving.
  • Direct Data Export: Allows immediate download and YOLO training label generation for computer vision and ALPR datasets.

3. Coordinate Mapping & Training Formulas

Let $(X_{v1}, Y_{v1}, X_{v2}, Y_{v2})$ denote the vehicle bounding box coordinates in the original frame. Let $(x_{p1}, y_{p1}, x_{p2}, y_{p2})$ denote the license plate coordinates within the vehicle crop: 

Global Plate Coordinate:
X_global = X_v1 + x_p1
Y_global = Y_v1 + y_p1

YOLO Training Format (Normalized [0.0 - 1.0]):
c_x = ((X1 + X2) / 2.0) / Image_Width
c_y = ((Y1 + Y2) / 2.0) / Image_Height
w   = (X2 - X1) / Image_Width
h   = (Y2 - Y1) / Image_Height
